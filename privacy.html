Privacy Policy

Aetheris: Sacred Wastes (圣脉荒原) · © 2026 Tactic Master · last updated November 2026

Aetheris does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts to create, no email address to give, and nothing is sent to the developer — or anywhere else. The only network traffic is the multiplayer game you choose to play, with the players you choose to play with.

No accountsNo analyticsNo advertising No in-app purchasesNo crash reportingNo tracking

1. Summary

Aetheris runs on your own Mac. It has no sign-in of its own, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or telemetry library, and no crash or error reporting. It contains no in-app purchases. Single-player play, including the tutorial, works with the network switched off.

2. What is stored on your device

The game writes a few files to its own data folder, inside the container macOS gives every App Store app. They stay on your Mac and are never uploaded:

  • Settings — settings.cfg: language, volumes, controls, graphics and interface choices, and which one-time tips you have already seen.
  • Saved games — the saves folder: the games you save, and the automatic saves made at the end of each turn.

Deleting the game removes them. There are no cookies and no identifier of any kind.

3. Network connections

The game opens a network connection only for multiplayer, and only when you ask for it:

  • LAN games. Hosting a game listens on your local network (port 47321), and the LAN screen announces and finds games with small broadcast messages on your local network (port 47322) — just a fixed game identifier and the port to join, nothing about you. This traffic stays on your local network. macOS asks your permission first.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, the game reports the achievements you unlock and, when you win by conquest, how many rounds it took (for the leaderboard). If you play online, Apple's Game Center finds the other player and carries the game between you. That is Apple's service, under Apple's privacy policy; Aetheris receives only your Game Center display name, to show it to the other player in the match.

What goes between players in a match is the game itself: the map, the moves, and the player names. Nothing of it is kept after the match, and nothing is sent to the developer.

4. Third parties

No analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or telemetry SDK is included. The only third-party service is Apple Game Center, and only when you sign in to it, as described above.

5. Children

Aetheris collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Online play goes through Game Center, whose own parental controls apply.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
