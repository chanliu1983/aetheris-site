Support

Aetheris: Sacred Wastes (圣脉荒原) · a mech strategy game of water, power and steel

Found a problem, or stuck somewhere? Open an issue — every one is read.

Report a problem

It helps to say which version you play (shown at the bottom of the main menu), your device and system version, what you were doing, and — if you can — attach a save from the moment it happened.

Getting started

Where do I begin?
Choose Tutorial on the main menu. A small hand-made map walks you through one thing at a time — founding, water, farms, power, a hangar, moving and firing, research, Jet Jump and the vein victory — and each step waits until you have done it. The pause menu can skip or replay it, and the Field Guide and Codex explain every rule.
How does the campaign work?
Main menu → Campaign. Ten missions in three acts (the Order, the Tribes, the Watchers) and a finale where you choose your culture. Each mission has an objective and two star challenges shown on screen (press O to fold the panel); missions unlock in order, and you can replay any you've won for more stars.
How do I win?
Two ways. Conquest: capture every fortress of every rival. The vein: lay a pipe to the ancient vein nodes and pump at least two of them, more than any single rival, then hold that lead for 20 cycles in a row. Everyone sees the countdown, so expect company. Hover the flag on the top bar to see where each side stands.
Why can't my mech use Jet Jump (or any skill)?
Every unit skill must be researched first. Press T (or click the science button on the top bar) and research Thrusters for Jet Jump. Research comes from powered Aether Labs and from the vein nodes you hold. Skills also cost energy, which your power grid recharges.
My mech is overheating.
Mechs cool from your water network. Next to your own pipes or town, with enough water, they refill at the end of the turn; away from it they lose a little coolant every turn, and at zero they deal less damage and lose range. Lay a pipe toward the front, or research Field Systems for Coolant Flush (once every 5 rounds per mech).
Why can't I build here?
Point at the spot — the reason is shown. The usual ones: an enemy unit on it or next to it (drive it off first), not enough craft this cycle, no free capacity (water another farm), or a district too far from the fortress core.
How do I end a turn?
Click End (or press Enter). If units still have moves, End first steps to each of them; Shift-click End (or Shift+Enter) ends the turn at once.

Multiplayer

A LAN game doesn't show up on the other device.
Both devices need the same version of the game and the same network. Allow Aetheris to find devices on the local network when asked. Some guest or office Wi-Fi networks keep devices apart.
Can I play with friends online?
Yes, through Game Center: open LAN on the main menu, sign in to Game Center, and choose Play online (Game Center) to find an opponent or invite a friend.

Controls and settings

Can I play on iPhone or iPad?
Yes. The full game includes a touch-first interface and landscape play on iPhone and iPad, with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
Does it work with a controller?
Yes, every screen does. In play: the left stick moves the cursor, A acts, RT selects your next unit, LT / RT pick a selected unit's skills, Y ends the turn, pressing the right stick opens the science tree, and Start opens the menu.
How do I change the language?
Settings → Language: English or 中文. The first launch follows your device's language.
Where are my saves?
Inside the game's own container on your device — see the privacy policy. The game also saves automatically at the end of every turn; Continue on the main menu picks up the latest one. Nothing is stored online.
